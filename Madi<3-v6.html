LOST LOTUS LULLABY

What time so loved, in shine and song so bloomed,
Blunt winter dare frost those petals cold.
What time did form, to dust he’d sold.
Bright petals turn dull, fall like wind so plumed.

Hearts so far, lost to reach, where petals fall, one to each, foul winter breath so loomed.
To whisper death, withered petals did, he’d told.
Flower bloomed from fates our own, but was time’s alone to hold.
Unwilling wilt assumed.

But try time he might, and though he will, I’ll spite each winter’s icy chill.
So, Father Time doth our hearts eschew,
For no greater adoration does time, for you, in me instill.
That no distance, nor time so cold, could dare halt the beating of my heart for two.

In knowing, I’ll hold you in my arms until,
For each fallen petal I’ll in time renew.

To my beloved Madilyn Swavola,
love Gabe Crocker

REVERIE UPON A HILL

Upon that golden sky my gaze doth rest, What gentle breeze sways earth, this grassy hill, myself too. How sun fills these eyes, warms this skin caressed. Who, all sun all sight be seized, might yet touch warmly this heart, but you? Indeed to challenge sun for reign, For all before me dare not bring greater bliss, What a view it is, but in your smile I might truly gain, Here to hold, exist there no greater scene than this. Oh to breathe, to touch, to live this sight, Find here no care, but for breeze and that beside. One with world, and one with light. I’ll gladly close these eyes, so might we hold eternal here, where love and peace reside. Why accursed sun must you rise today, mock these eyes with light, what vice? Again, to tear away from dream, away that paradise.

KILLERS

What a unique cruelty you now my mind infect, To promise promises, provoke the will, How might your poison, learn to love me still; The hour of our own, I tenderly reflect. So grab me by my heart, and all the doubts reject, Or crueler would I be, if I’d fulfill, My foolish thoughts, to meet I first must kill; This choice steals choice, that ends with mine and yours collect. Who is crueler? But what’s it matter, if my heart still hurts for you. My greedy heart, I hate, will ask for all you have, but knows it can’t, Which is more than you can give, or could, and I’m the killer, My words as poison to you now as yours were me, I do. Please, love the us we’ve met and lived in time, that yearn your certain pillar, No asks, no pleads, have trust in love for love each other, More than you can give, I’d never ask another, Cruel, but not unique, for I’m the crueler lover, When faith lies short, let love become the thriller. Please, poison me, and I can you, my dear; Please learn to love your killer.
